# 1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

## What is Sarfarosh?

**Sarfarosh** is a performing-arts-led creative and event organization founded by **Yash**, an actor and filmmaker.

Sarfarosh began as a performing-arts team focused on communicating social issues and public messages through live performance. Its work has included **nukkad natak, drama, singing, dancing, flash mobs, and other performing-art formats**.

Over time, Sarfarosh developed a broader ecosystem around live experiences, creative production, public engagement, entertainment, brand work, and event execution.

The next stage of the organization is to establish Sarfarosh as a **professional event management and event production company**, while retaining its strongest differentiator: its roots in performance, audience engagement, and live execution.

### Core positioning

**EVENT MANAGEMENT • EXPERIENCES • PRODUCTION • ACTIVATIONS • ENTERTAINMENT**

### Core brand idea

> **We don't just organize events. We create experiences people remember.**

### Strategic positioning

> **Built on performance. Engineered for events.**

Sarfarosh should be presented as a company capable of handling an event from **concept to execution**, including creative, production, entertainment, logistics, audience engagement, and on-ground operations.

# 20. EVENT EXPERIENCE MODEL

Sarfarosh should define an event lifecycle:

## 01 — DISCOVER

Understand the client's objective, audience, location, budget, timeline, and desired outcome.

## 02 — CONCEPT

Develop the event concept, creative direction, experience design, and programming.

## 03 — PLAN

Build the production plan, operations plan, staffing plan, vendor plan, logistics plan, and timeline.

## 04 — PRODUCE

Execute creative, technical, infrastructure, artist, and operational requirements.

## 05 — EXECUTE

Manage the live event, teams, guests, artists, production, and audience experience.

## 06 — DELIVER

Complete documentation, content, reporting, post-event analysis, and client handover.
